National Merit Scholarship (Academic Session 2026-27)

Department of Higher Education & Welfare | Official Gazette Notification

Published: 01 August 2026 | Last Updated: 15 September 2026

1. Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must be bonafide citizens enrolled in full-time undergraduate study at an accredited university. Eligible candidates must have achieved a minimum aggregate of 75% marks in their Higher Secondary Examination (Class 12). Total gross family income from all sources must not exceed INR 4,50,000 per annum.

2. Mandatory Documentation

Candidates are required to upload digital copies of their Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, an official Domicile Certificate issued by a competent magistrate, and an authenticated Income Certificate issued exclusively by the Revenue Officer / Tehsildar or a valid Ration Card.

3. Key Deadlines and Schedules

The online application portal opens on 15 August 2026. The definitive closing date for submitting online applications is 30 November 2026 at 11:59 PM IST. Incomplete submissions or submissions made after the closing date will be rejected automatically without notice.

4. Award Amount & Disbursement

Selected scholarship recipients will receive a non-repayable grant of INR 50,000 per academic year, transferred directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the recipient's Aadhaar-seeded bank account in two equal biannual installments.

5. Official Application Procedure

Applications must be submitted solely through the designated official web portal at https://scholarships.gov.in/national-merit-2026. No physical applications or email submissions will be entertained.
