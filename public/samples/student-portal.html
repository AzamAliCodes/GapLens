StudentDesk: Complete Breakdown of the National Merit Scholarship 2026

By Editorial Team | Last updated: 20 September 2026

Overview & Qualifying Standards

The National Merit Scholarship provides generous financial aid to undergraduate students. Applicants need to show at least 75% marks in their 12th board exams and must have household income within the Rs 4.5 Lakh ceiling.

Required Documents Checklist

Make sure you have your Class 12 passing certificate, student ID card, and bank passbook ready. For financial verification, applicants must upload their parent's formal Income Tax Return (ITR) acknowledgment from the past assessment year or Form 16 from their employer. Revenue certificates without ITR are not accepted on the portal.

Application Window

The official portal opened in mid-August and the last date for submitting online applications is 30 November 2026.
