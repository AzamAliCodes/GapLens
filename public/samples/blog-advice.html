EduTips Blog: Tips for the National Merit Scholarship

Archived Post | Academic Session 2024-25 batch

Eligibility Details

For the AY 2024-25 cycle, the ministry requires students to maintain a consistent academic record with 75% marks in high school exams. Ensure your college is enrolled under the National Scholarship Portal.

Application Strategy

Be aware that the portal for the 2024-25 batch closed in October 2024. Students should always verify whether previous cycle rules carry over to the new term.
